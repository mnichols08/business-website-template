### 1. Purpose
The goal of this Code of Conduct is to help {{COMMUNITY_NAME}} foster an open, safe, inclusive, and productive environment for everyone participating in {{PROJECT_NAME}}—whether in issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, real-time chats, events, or any other project space.

### 2. Scope
This Code applies to all project spaces managed by the {{PROJECT_NAME}} maintainers and to interactions in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or community. Representation includes using an official email, posting via an official social media account, or explicitly acting as a designated community representative.

### 3. Our Standards
Positive behaviors that help create a better environment include:
* Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward others.
* Being inclusive of differing viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
* Showing gratitude and recognition for contributions of all sizes.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.

Unacceptable behaviors include (non-exhaustive):
* Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory comments (including slurs) related to gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, mental or physical health, neurotype, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion (or lack thereof), nationality, or technology choices.
* Threats or incitement of violence, or encouraging self-harm.
* Deliberate intimidation, stalking, doxxing, or sustained disruption of discussions.
* Unwanted sexual attention or advances.
* Publication of others’ private information without explicit permission.
* Excessive rudeness, trolling, baiting, or inflammatory political or religious arguments unrelated to the project.
* Any behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### 4. Additional Standards for Maintainers
Maintainers and members of {{SAFETY_TEAM_NAME}} are expected to model healthy communication, apply this Code consistently, and recuse themselves from decisions where they have a conflict of interest. Failure to uphold these standards may result in temporary or permanent loss of maintainer status.

### 5. Reporting a Concern
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior—or have other concerns—please report it as soon as possible using one of the following channels:
* Email: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}
* Web/Form (optional): {{CONTACT_URL}}

You may include as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing. Helpful details include:
* Your contact info (if comfortable) for follow-up.
* Names or handles of those involved.
* Date, time (with timezone), and location/context (URL, channel) of incident(s).
* Any relevant screenshots, logs, or links.
* Whether the incident is ongoing.

Reports will be handled as promptly and discreetly as practicable. We will not publicly name reporters without explicit consent.

### 6. Enforcement & Process
The {{SAFETY_TEAM_NAME}} (or designated maintainers) will review each report. A typical workflow may include:
1. Acknowledge receipt within 2 business days ({{TIMEZONE}}) when feasible.
2. Assess immediate safety concerns and take interim action if needed.
3. Conduct a fact-finding review (may involve confidentially contacting involved parties).
4. Decide on an appropriate response based on this Code.
5. Communicate the decision to relevant parties while preserving privacy where possible.

### 7. Possible Consequences
Actions taken in response to violations may include (non-exhaustive, escalating):
* Informal warning / educational guidance.
* Formal written warning with expectations outlined.
* Temporary moderation (e.g., limited posting privileges).
* Temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
* Removal of maintainer / contributor roles.
* Reporting to hosting provider, event venue, or (if legally required) law enforcement.

The response will be proportionate to the severity and frequency of the behavior.

### 8. Confidentiality
We will treat reports with sensitivity. Information is shared only with those who need it to address the report. Anonymous reports may limit our ability to take direct action but will still be evaluated for pattern detection and systemic improvements.

### 9. Conflict of Interest
If a report concerns a member of the {{SAFETY_TEAM_NAME}} or a maintainer, that individual will be excluded from the review process. If all current reviewers are conflicted, an external neutral party may be requested to assist.

### 10. Appeals
If you believe a decision was made in error or handled unfairly, you may appeal within 14 days by emailing {{DECISION_APPEAL_EMAIL}} with: (a) original decision summary (if available), (b) basis for appeal (new information, procedural issue, disproportionate consequence). An independent review will be conducted where feasible; the appeal decision is final.

### 11. Retaliation
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation of this Code.
